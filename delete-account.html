Delete your account

StayWell · Share the Load

In the app

The fastest way is from the app itself. Deleting your account there happens right away.

  • iPhone: More → Settings → your name → Delete Account
  • Android: More → Settings → your name → Delete Account

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm it's you, then delete it within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

support@staywellsharetheload.com

What gets deleted

Your profile, household, meal plan, cookbook, shopping lists, pantry, deliveries and subscriptions, chores, calendar, scanned recipe photos, photos you added to items, feedback you've sent, and notification settings are permanently deleted from our servers. It can't be undone.

  • If you own a household that other people belong to, they need to leave (or be removed) first, since deleting the household would delete their data too.
  • If you're a member of someone else's household, the items you added there, such as recipes, chores and shopping items, stay with that household.
  • Recipes you've already shared with someone may stay available to them.
  • Deleting your account doesn't cancel a StayWell Pro subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings (iPhone) or the Google Play Store (Android).
