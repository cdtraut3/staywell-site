Subscriptions and billing

Stay Well Pro is billed and managed entirely through Apple. For refunds, billing disputes, or to cancel a subscription, use Apple's own support tools — we don't have access to your payment information and can't process refunds directly:

  • Manage or cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad
  • Refund requests: reportaproblem.apple.com

Bug reports and feedback

You can also send feedback directly from the app: Settings → About → Send Feedback.
