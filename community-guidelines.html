Recipe Sharing Guidelines

Last updated: November 4, 2026

StayWell lets you share recipes from your cookbook with friends and family, by text message or however you like to share. Our goal is for every recipe shared through StayWell to be one people can confidently share and enjoy. These guidelines explain what's okay to share.

How sharing works

StayWell shares recipes in a way that respects the people who wrote them:

  • Recipes imported from a website are shared as their title, the website's name, the ingredient list and a link to the original page. The author's photos, story and instructions stay on their site, and whoever you share with goes there for the full recipe.
  • Recipes you wrote yourself are shared in full. If you adapted one from somewhere, add the source link so the original gets credit.
  • Recipes scanned from a photo, sent to you by someone else, or saved before November 2026 are shared as their title and ingredients only, since the steps may be someone else's words. If one is your own (say, a family recipe card), you can mark it as your own recipe in the app to share it in full.

Your notes and any private steps StayWell wrote for you are never shared.

Marking a recipe as your own

Only mark a recipe as your own if you created it, it's a family recipe you have the right to share, or you have permission from its owner or publisher. Please don't use it for recipes copied from websites, blogs, meal kit services, cookbooks, magazines or other sources.

Reporting a concern

If you discover content that you believe infringes your rights, or otherwise shouldn't be shared, please contact us so we can review it promptly. See our Copyright Policy for how to submit a copyright notice.
