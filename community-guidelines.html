Community Recipe Guidelines

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Our goal is to build a collection of recipes our community can confidently share and enjoy. These guidelines explain what's okay to publish to the StayWell Cookbook, the public, shared recipe collection inside the app.

What you can upload

Please only upload recipes that:

  • You created yourself.
  • Are family recipes that you have the right to share.
  • You have permission from the owner or publisher to share publicly.

What not to upload

Please do not upload recipes copied from websites, blogs, meal kit services, cookbooks, Pinterest links, magazines, or other sources unless you have permission to publish them.

Recipes imported into your private cookbook from external sources are intended to help you organize your own personal collection. They should not be shared with others through Stay Well unless you have the appropriate rights or permission.

Before you share

Whenever you publish a recipe to the community cookbook, or send one directly to another person, Stay Well will ask you to confirm that you created the recipe or have permission to share it. Answering honestly keeps this a collection people can trust.

Reporting a concern

If you discover content that you believe infringes your rights, or otherwise shouldn't be here, please contact us so we can review it promptly. See our Copyright Policy for how to submit a copyright notice.
